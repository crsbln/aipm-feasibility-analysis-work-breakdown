## Quality Check

- [ ] The hierarchy represents all approved project scope and no extra scope.
- [ ] Child elements represent 100% of each parent element.
- [ ] Branches do not duplicate the same work.
- [ ] Work packages are bounded, ownable, and verifiable.
- [ ] Functional and non-functional requirements are traceable.
- [ ] Operations, evaluation, compliance, rollout, and project management are
      included where relevant.
- [ ] Exclusions, assumptions, dependencies, and open questions are explicit.
